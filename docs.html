01What host is

host launches pump.fun coins inside other coins. You pick a host, and your coin's bonding curve is priced in that host instead of SOL. Every buy of your coin pays in the host, so every buy of yours is a buy of the host too.

It answers PvP. When a narrative hits, fifty copies launch and split the buyers. On host, a copy can launch inside the original: the copy's buyers feed the coin that started it, instead of draining it.

pump.fun's own program does the work. pump.fun's program can price a new coin in another asset. host gives that a front door: a host picker, a checker, one transaction, and a public list of every coin launched this way.

02Launch a coin

  1. Pick a host. Tap one from pump.fun's list or paste any contract. host asks pump.fun's program whether it can host, and how deep your coin would sit.
  2. Add your coin. An image (cropped square in your browser), a name, a ticker, a description and links.
  3. Choose a first buy, or none. The first buy is paid in the host coin, from your wallet. host shows how much of the host you hold.
  4. Launch. host stores the image and details as your coin's metadata, builds the transaction and tries it against Solana. Your wallet signs, then the page signs for the new coin's address, and the launch is sent.
  5. Live. When Solana confirms, your coin is on pump.fun, priced in the host, and on the guest list.

03The transaction

One transaction, built with pump.fun's official SDK:

  • Compute budget. The limit is set from a simulation of this exact launch, plus a margin.
  • create_v2 on pump.fun's program, with the host as the quote mint. This makes the coin, its metadata and its curve.
  • buy_v2, only if you chose a first buy. It pays in the host from your wallet's host account.
  • A 0 SOL transfer to host's tag address, plus a memo reading host:<your coin>:<the host> when it fits in the transaction. That's how the guest list finds launches on chain. It moves no money.

Two keys sign it: your wallet (it pays) and the new coin's address. That address is a key made in your browser for this launch. It signs once and is never sent anywhere.

Before your wallet sees anything, host simulates the transaction on Solana mainnet. If it would fail, you get the reason and nothing is sent.

04Which coins can host

Two kinds of coins can host:

  • Assets on pump.fun's host list. pump.fun keeps a list on chain of assets a coin can be priced in: a few hundred right now, including crypto on Solana (ETH, BTC, XRP, SUI and more through their Solana versions), big memes and tokenized stocks. host reads it live.
  • Any pump coin, within pump.fun's limits: it can't be too deep already, can't be in mayhem mode, and can't be moving to PumpSwap at that moment.

When a coin can't host, the checker says why, in pump.fun's terms: not a pump coin and not on the list, too deep, not eligible, migrating, price out of range, or a graduated coin whose pool wasn't found.

SOL isn't a host: every normal pump.fun coin is already priced in SOL. BNB and other chains aren't on pump.fun's list.

05Depth

Depth counts how many coins sit between yours and the base. A coin priced in SOL or in a listed asset is a root coin, at depth 0. A coin launched inside a root coin is at depth 1. A coin inside that one is at depth 2, and so on.

pump.fun's program caps it. Right now the cap is 5, read live from pump.fun's global settings. A buy at the bottom of a chain moves every coin above it.

06Buying and graduating

Your coin trades on pump.fun's curve, and the curve takes the host coin. A buyer pays with the host, or swaps SOL into the host on the way in. Either way, every buy of your coin is a buy of the host, and those host coins sit in your curve.

A full curve graduates to PumpSwap into a pool paired with the host coin, so your chart and the host's move together from then on.

The pair is set at launch and can't change.

07Costs

  • host takes nothing. No fee, no cut of your coin.
  • About 0.01 SOL for the new accounts and network fees. The app shows the figure from the simulation before you sign.
  • Your first buy, if you make one, paid in the host coin.
  • pump.fun's trading fees apply to every trade, as on any pump coin.

08Safety

  • host never holds a key or your money. The server builds unsigned transactions and relays signed ones. It has no wallet of its own.
  • Your wallet signs one thing: the launch you built. host never asks for your recovery phrase.
  • The new coin's key is made in your browser, signs the launch once, and never leaves the page.
  • host only relays its own launches. The send endpoint refuses any transaction that doesn't call pump.fun's program and carry host's tag.
  • No cookies, no analytics. The pages get their data from host's own server.

A coin's price can go to zero. A host can fall, and a coin priced in it falls with it. Nothing on host is advice.

09The guest list

Every launch through host sends 0 SOL to host's tag address. The guest list reads that address's history from Solana, keeps the launches that called pump.fun's program, reads the coin and its host from each one, and adds names, images and curve progress.

Anyone can check it: the tag address on Solscan ↗.

10API

  • GET /api/hosts: pump.fun's host list, with names, icons and prices from Jupiter.
  • GET /api/check?mint=: can this coin host, where it comes from, and the depth a coin inside it would have.
  • GET /api/list: every coin launched through host.
  • GET /api/config: the live depth cap and the tag address.
  • POST /api/meta: stores a coin's image and metadata.
  • POST /api/build, /api/send, GET /api/status: the launch, built, sent and watched.

11FAQ

Is host pump.fun?
No. host is built on pump.fun's program and launches real pump.fun coins, but it isn't run by pump.fun.

Do I need the host coin to launch?
Only for a first buy. A launch with no first buy needs only SOL for the fees.

Can I launch inside my own coin?
Yes, if it can host. Paste it in the checker.

Can a host stop my coin?
No. Once launched, your coin is a normal pump.fun coin priced in the host. Nobody can change the pair.
