D1THE PROBLEM

Fifty copies. One narrative. Everyone loses.

A narrative hits and fifty coins launch with the same name. They split the buyers, the liquidity and the attention. Most die in an hour. That's PvP.

PVPON HOST
⇆

Without host: every copy is a rival. A buy of one is a sell of the others, and the original bleeds.

On host: the copies launch inside the original. Every buy of a copy buys the original first. The narrative's coin gets fed, not farmed.

D2HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. One door.

01 · PICK A HOST

Pick the coin you launch inside.

Any pump coin, or a listed asset: ETH, BTC, XRP, SUI, SPX6900, PUMP, even stocks like NVDA. Paste a contract and host checks it.

02 · LAUNCH INSIDE

Your coin is priced in the host.

One transaction creates your coin on pump.fun with its curve priced in the host coin, not in SOL. The pair is set for good.

03 · EVERY BUY FEEDS IT

Buying yours means buying the host.

Buyers pay in the host, or pay SOL and it's swapped into the host on the way in. The host coins sit in your curve.

04 · GRADUATE TOGETHER

Graduate into a pool with the host.

When your curve fills, your coin moves to PumpSwap in a pool paired with the host. Your chart and the host's move together.

D10QUESTIONS

Before you go inside.

What is host?

A launchpad for pump.fun coins that are priced inside another coin. Pick a host, launch inside it, and every buy of your coin goes through the host first.

Which coins can be a host?

Any pump coin, and every asset on pump.fun's quote list: crypto like ETH, BTC, XRP, SUI and SPX6900 through their Solana versions, big memes like WIF and BONK, and tokenized stocks. BNB coins aren't on the list yet.

How does it end PvP?

A copy launched inside the original isn't its rival. Every buy of the copy buys the original first, so the coin that started the narrative gets fed by every coin that follows it.

How do people buy my coin?

On pump.fun, with the host coin, or with SOL that gets swapped into the host on the way in. Your coin page has a link straight to it.

Can I change the host later?

No. The host is part of your coin's curve from the first block, and it's what your coin pairs with when it graduates.

How deep can coins go?

Five. A coin inside a pump coin can host a coin of its own, and so on, up to pump.fun's limit of five.

What does it cost?

host takes nothing. A launch costs a little SOL for the new accounts and network fees, plus your first buy if you make one. pump.fun's trading fees apply as on any pump coin.

Is this pump.fun?

host is built on pump.fun's own program and launches real pump.fun coins. It isn't run by pump.fun.
