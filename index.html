01The problem

Fifty copies. One narrative. Everyone loses.

A narrative hits and fifty coins launch with the same name. They split the buyers, the liquidity and the attention, and most are dead in an hour. That's PvP. Drag the line to compare.

PVPON HOST
⇆

Without host: every copy is a rival. A buy of one is money the others don't get, and the original bleeds.

On host: the copies launch inside the original. Every buy of a copy buys the original first, so the coin that started it gets fed.

11Questions

Before you launch.

What is host?

A launchpad for pump.fun coins that are priced inside another coin. Pick a host, launch inside it, and every buy of your coin goes through the host first.

Which coins can be a host?

Any pump coin, and every asset on pump.fun's quote list: crypto like ETH, BTC, XRP, SUI and SPX6900 through their Solana versions, big memes like WIF and BONK, and tokenized stocks. BNB coins aren't on the list yet. Use the checker to ask about any coin.

How does it end PvP?

A copy launched inside the original isn't its rival. Every buy of the copy buys the original first, so the coin that started the narrative gets fed by every coin that follows it.

How do people buy my coin?

Your coin trades on pump.fun's curve, and that curve takes the host coin. Buyers pay with the host, or swap SOL into the host on the way in, so every buy of your coin is a buy of the host.

Can I change the host later?

No. The host is part of your coin's curve from the first block, and it's what your coin pairs with when it graduates.

How deep can coins go?

pump.fun sets the limit in its program, and host reads it live: right now coins can sit five levels deep inside other coins.

What does it cost?

host takes nothing. A launch costs about 0.01 SOL for the new accounts and network fees, plus your first buy if you make one. pump.fun's trading fees apply as on any pump coin.

Is this pump.fun?

host is built on pump.fun's own program and launches real pump.fun coins. It isn't run by pump.fun.
